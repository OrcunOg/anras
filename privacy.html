Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026. This policy covers the Anras website, the Tall True Tales channel and Tall True Tales Studyo, the small publishing tool Anras uses to post its own videos.

Who we are

Anras is an independent creator studio. It makes Unity asset packs and runs the Tall True Tales short-video channel on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (@talltruetales). Contact: anrasofficial@gmail.com.

Tall True Tales Studyo

Tall True Tales Studyo is a private tool that runs on the Anras team's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and the only accounts that sign in to it are the Tall True Tales accounts owned by Anras. It uploads videos that Anras made to those accounts and shows whether each post went through.

When an Anras account is connected, the tool receives only what it needs for that job:

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and to read basic channel information such as the channel name and the status of uploaded videos.
  • Instagram (Meta): basic account and linked Facebook Page information, and permission to publish Reels.
  • TikTok: basic profile information such as the display name and avatar, and permission to upload or publish videos.

Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Anras, not sold and not shared with anyone. Video files and captions go only to the platform they are being posted to. The tool has no analytics or advertising code.

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be removed at any time from the platform side: Google account permissions, Facebook and Instagram "Apps and websites" settings, and TikTok "Manage app permissions".

Tall True Tales Studyo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers and visitors

Anras does not collect personal data from people who watch Tall True Tales videos. Views, likes and comments are handled by YouTube, Instagram and TikTok under their own privacy policies. This website does not use cookies or analytics; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: anrasofficial@gmail.com.
